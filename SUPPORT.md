# Exawatt Support

**TL;DR: Use Discussions for questions, issues for reproducible defects, and private advisories for security reports.**

Choose the channel that matches the outcome:

- **Setup, usage, and extension questions:** use
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Full-Vibe/exawatt/discussions).
- **Reproducible product defects:** use the bug report issue form.
- **Roadmap or feature proposals:** use the roadmap proposal form or a
  Discussion before implementation.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** report privately under
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of Conduct reports:** follow the private reporting instructions in
  [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Search existing issues and Discussions first. When asking for help, include the
commit or version, macOS version, official/community/custom distribution,
Agent Source, Demo or Live mode, exact steps, expected behavior, and sanitized
logs. Remove credentials, tokens, private prompts, personal data, repository
names, and customer information.

Community source builds and downstream distributions are community-supported.
Their distributor owns its service configuration, signing identity, update
channel, and support promises. Official Exawatt distributions identify their
support route in the product or official download surface.

Maintainers prioritize roadmap work and actionable defects and cannot promise
individual response times. Please do not request support through personal
messages to contributors.

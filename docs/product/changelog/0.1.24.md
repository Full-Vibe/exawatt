title: Your shell settings, under every shell
---

If you use fish or tcsh, Exawatt now finds the agent home folders your shell
sets, such as CODEX_HOME or CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR. In 0.1.22 that worked only
in zsh, bash and sh. A value that spans several lines now arrives whole.

## Improvements

- Starting a Codex agent no longer waits for Exawatt to read your shell
  settings.

## Fixes

- Quit, Restart and Restart to Update always finish, or tell you what they
  are still waiting for.
- Cancel appears only while cancelling can still keep every agent running.
- If macOS finishes preparing an update after Exawatt said it could not, the
  notice offers Restart to Update again.
- After a restart or update, something that cannot come back no longer
  stops the rest from coming back.

Usage shows how fast your agents are spending right now, warns you before a
plan runs out, and adds your Google account beside Claude and Codex.

## New

- **A live burn line.** Tokens per minute and dollars per hour over the last
  ten minutes, for each account.
- **Run-out alerts.** A notification when a plan is on course to run out
  before it resets. Turn them off in Settings.
- **Your Google account on Usage.** Weekly limits for each model group, read
  from your own Antigravity CLI.
- **Antigravity CLI from Google.** Launch and resume its agents like any
  other.
- **Fleet v2, as a Preview.** A new Fleet board with five materials. Turn it
  on in Settings; the current board stays the default.

## Improvements

- The Fleet board keeps moving on battery, at a slower pace, instead of
  freezing.
- Sent feedback shows it was sent and closes itself.

## Fixes

- Restart to Update has macOS accept the update before any agent stops, and
  a refused or failed install no longer hangs on "Closing Exawatt…".
